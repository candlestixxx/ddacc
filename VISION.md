# DDACC Vision & Core Ethos

## The Ultimate Goal
The **Detroit Digital Artist Convention & Conference (DDACC)** is a hybrid premier physical and digital summit that bridges Michigan's storied industrial and underground art traditions with modern digital craftsmanship.

DDACC rejects the impersonal, speculative, and sterile tech conference paradigm. Instead, it is built upon **The Love Collective**:
1. **Radical Community Ownership**: Tools, tutorials, work-in-progress files, and knowledge are shared openly.
2. **Industrial Grit & High-Tech Expression**: Celebrating Detroit's unique aesthetic—from techno synthesizer visuals and automotive concept CAD to 3D sculpting, XR installations, shaders, and open-source creative suites (Blender, Godot, Krita).
3. **Equitable Artist Compensation**: Transparent table fees, travel stipends for underrepresented creators, and live portfolio clinics led by senior industry artists.

## Experience Architecture
- **Unified Command Dashboard**: Attendees, exhibitors, and remote participants navigate a consolidated, high-density dashboard rather than scattered portal silos.
- **Physical-Digital Bridging**: Real-time collaborative digital canvas, live projection sync, and responsive physical-space schedules mapped directly to Huntington Place Detroit and synchronized globally via web.
- **Persistent Systems Operations**: Embedded system tray controls providing transparent visibility into server heartbeat, projection stream health, and interactive nodes.
