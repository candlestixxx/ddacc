# VISION.md

"To empower, elevate, and connect digital creators across Detroit and beyond by bridging the gap between artistic community and industry innovation. DDACC is a collaborative platform dedicated to celebrating digital craftsmanship, championing creative equity, and shaping the future of digital art."

## The Love Collective Ethos
Bringing a creative community together around digital art via shared vision, effective platforming, and strong community engagement.
Focusing on:
- Collaborative art drops
- Open-source creation
- Supporting emerging digital creators
- Hosting virtual/physical showcases
