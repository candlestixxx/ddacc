# DDACC

DDACC (Detroit Digital Arts & Creators Collective) — a platform concept for
connecting digital creators in Detroit and beyond: collaborative art drops,
open-source creation, support for emerging digital creators, and
virtual/physical showcases. The stated vision is to bridge the gap between
artistic community and industry innovation.

## Status

**Parked / early concept.** The repo was created 2026-09-29 and has exactly
4 commits; the last one is **2026-09-30** (a PR merge of the initial core
platform: landing page, schedule, portal, and digital wall). Nothing has
been committed since. The nonprofit (501c3) idea associated with DDACC is
