Detroit Digital Art Conference fits the vision of bringing creative communities and digital creators together. Detroit’s art and design scene—especially around UNESCO’s Design City designation—makes it a natural fit for an event like that.

Here is the difference between calling an event a Conference versus a Convention:

Feature	Conference	Convention
Core Focus	Professional, academic, or industry-driven	Fan-driven, community-oriented, or trade-focused
Main Activities	Keynotes, panel discussions, workshops, networking	Artist alleys, exhibitions, cosplay, merch, meetups
Atmosphere	Educational, formal, focused on ideas/skills	Social, festive, celebratory, consumer-facing
Target Audience	Working digital artists, developers, educators	Hobbyists, fans, creators, general public
Which term fits DDAC better?
Choose "Conference" if: The goal is professional development, panels on digital tools/art theory, emerging tech (like generative tools or 3D pipeline), and industry networking.

Choose "Convention" (or Expo) if: The goal is a massive creative showcase, an open artist marketplace, live digital painting battles, and consumer-facing exhibitions.

Many modern events use "Festival" or "Summit" if they want to bridge the gap—balancing professional talks with an interactive, community-led celebration.
