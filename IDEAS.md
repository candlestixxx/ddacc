# Aggressive Ideas & Creative Innovations for DDACC

## 1. Physical Hardware & Venue Innovations
- **Detroit Techno Visualizer Stage**: Connect real-time MIDI/OSC signals from Detroit electronic musicians directly to the live Digital Wall projections via TouchDesigner or WebGL canvas.
- **Physical LED Table Beacons**: Provide battery-operated ESP32 LED beacons for physical artist tables that illuminate when an online convention attendee bookmarks their showcase.
- **Arcade Showcase Pod**: Retrofit vintage arcade cabinets with Raspberry Pi / Linux units running indie Detroit game developer demos.

## 2. Platform Architecture Innovations
- **P2P Ephemeral Art Canvas**: Use WebRTC data channels for direct artist-to-artist collaborative sketching without routing canvas strokes through central servers.
- **Decentralized Archive on IPFS/Arweave**: Store all submitted digital wall artwork and exhibition recordings permanently on decentralized storage so the cultural output of DDACC is never lost.
- **Micro-Grants & Direct Tips**: Implement direct tipping links (Stripe / WebMonetization / CashApp) on artist cards so attendees can directly support creators without platform commissions.
